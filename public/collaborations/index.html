How we work together

What Ie offers

The rooms. The rhythm of the house. The Atelier. The people who come to the house. Documentation of the work.

What Ie asks

Time, and specificity. Respect for the building: no nails, no fixings to old timber or earthen walls, limits on noise and light. [house rules TO CONFIRM] And work that would only make sense here.

First season: in development. There are no past projects to show yet.
