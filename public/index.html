Ie Fushimi

The IeFushimi

You feel Ie before you see it.

Ie Fushimi is a house of tea in an old Kyoto machiya near Fushimi Inari.

The house came first. Its timber, earth, wells, shadows and garden are not a backdrop — they are where we began.

The house was already speaking. We began by listening.

The House

Ie Atelier

Objects the house uses every day — commissioned, tested in the rooms, then made in small editions. Tea, ceramics, incense, copper, paper, light. Some made by the people who stay and work here. A way of carrying a piece of Ie home.
