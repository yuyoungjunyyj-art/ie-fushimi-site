Leaves are measured. Water is heated. Spices are crushed. Milk warms. Tea rests. A vessel is held. Someone waits. Someone drinks.

Sand hearth hojicha chai

What
Ie's hojicha chai, made over a hearth of heated sand.
How
A copper pot is set into hot sand. Roasted hojicha. Whole spices, crushed by hand. Milk warmed slowly. The sand gives an even heat, rather than a direct flame. [TO CONFIRM: spice list, milk options, sweetener, copper maker]
Why
Fire is water's counterpart. The hearth is the one piece of theatre the street can see. And it is tea that can be handed over straight away, to someone who has just walked down from the shrine.
Where in the house
The front of the house, to take away. [TO CONFIRM]
